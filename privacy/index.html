Privacy Policy

This policy describes what Joshua Israel Ventures LLC collects through Higher Math Guide. Effective date 8 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for this site. Higher Math Guide is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. It is not a separate company. Privacy requests go to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What this site collects

The site does not require an account. It does not ask you to create a profile. Calculator numbers stay in your browser. The site does not transmit those numbers to a server.

No advertising cookies and no affiliate cookies are used now. This policy will be updated before any advertising or affiliate cookies are added.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Each page includes a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. Cloudflare offers that tool as cookieless measurement. It reports aggregate visit information, such as page views, referrers, country, browser, and device type. It does not set a cookie on your browser for this measurement. We do not receive a list of named visitors from it.

Contact by email

The contact page does not use FormSubmit or any other form host. The form does not post your message to this website. With JavaScript enabled, choosing the button opens a draft in your own email program. The draft is addressed to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com with the subject [Contact: Higher Math Guide]. The draft body includes the name, email address, reason, optional topic, and message you typed. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. A mailto link on the same page opens the same address and subject with an empty body.

If you send the email, Joshua Israel Ventures LLC receives it at that inbox, including whatever you chose to write. We use it to read your note and, when we can, to reply. We do not sell that correspondence. We do not add you to a marketing list.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may keep ordinary server logs for security and operations. Those logs can include an IP address, the date and time of a request, the page requested, and the user agent sent with the request. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC does not operate a separate logging database for the site.

Children

The site is a general audience educational site. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 sent us an email, write to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com and ask us to delete it.

Your requests

Email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com to ask what email correspondence we have from you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. The site has no user accounts, so that correspondence is the personal information we hold from you. We will respond to a reasonable request.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, USA, without regard to conflict of law rules. Any dispute about it will be heard in the state or federal courts located in Florida.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will change. If advertising or affiliate cookies are added later, this policy will be updated before those cookies are used.
