Privacy Policy

This policy describes what Joshua Israel Ventures LLC collects through Higher Math Guide. Effective date 18 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for this site. Higher Math Guide is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. It is not a separate company. Privacy requests go through the contact form.

What this site collects

The site does not require an account. It does not ask you to create a profile. Calculator numbers stay in your browser. The site does not transmit those numbers to a server.

No advertising cookies and no affiliate cookies are used now. This policy will be updated before any advertising or affiliate cookies are added.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Each page includes a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. Cloudflare offers that tool as cookieless measurement. It reports aggregate visit information, such as page views, referrers, country, browser, and device type. It does not set a cookie on your browser for this measurement. We do not receive a list of named visitors from it.

Contact by email

The contact form posts your name, email address, and message to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers the note to the publisher. The subject is [Contact: highermathguide.com] New message. FormSubmit receives the fields you submit so it can deliver the message. The contact page does not show a mailbox address. After the form sends, the contact page shows a short thank you. An empty honeypot field and a short wait before sending are used instead of a captcha.

If you send the email, Joshua Israel Ventures LLC receives it at that inbox, including whatever you chose to write. We use it to read your note and, when we can, to reply. We do not sell that correspondence. We do not add you to a marketing list.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may keep ordinary server logs for security and operations. Those logs can include an IP address, the date and time of a request, the page requested, and the user agent sent with the request. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC does not operate a separate logging database for the site.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you can manage personalised ads in Google Ads Settings or opt out at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent choice before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used. California residents can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information by using these controls or by contacting us through the contact page.

Children

The site is a general audience educational site. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 sent a message, use the contact form and ask us to delete it.

Your requests

Use the contact form to ask what email correspondence we have from you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. The site has no user accounts, so that correspondence is the personal information we hold from you. We will respond to a reasonable request.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, USA, without regard to conflict of law rules. Any dispute about it will be heard in the state or federal courts located in Florida.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will change. If advertising or affiliate cookies are added later, this policy will be updated before those cookies are used.
