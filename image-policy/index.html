Image policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains how Higher Math Guide uses pictures. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC publishes the site. Questions about an image can be sent through the contact page.

AI generated photographs

The photographs and photo-style illustrations on this site are AI generated images. They are not photographs of real classrooms, chalkboards, or people as they appeared, and they are not pictures of a real lesson taking place. They are decorative illustrations that sit beside the explainers, topic maps, and calculators. A caption under each of these images says that it is an AI generated image or an illustrative AI generated photo and includes a Source link to this page.

Diagrams

Some pages also use teaching diagrams, such as geometric sketches of a determinant or a linear independence figure. Those diagrams are labelled in their captions. They are explanatory drawings, not photographs of events, and they are not a substitute for a formal proof.

Credits and questions

If a licensed or Creative Commons photograph is added later, its caption will name the source. Until then, treat every photo-style image on this site as an AI generated illustration and use this page as the source credit. If you have a question about an image, use the contact page.
