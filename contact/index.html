Contact

Send a question, a correction, or a topic idea. This is a free educational project of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, not a paid funnel.

What happens after you write

The form sends your name, email, and message. The subject is set to [Contact: highermathguide.com] New message. When it goes through, a thank you appears on this page. If the browser sends the form without JavaScript, you return here with the same thank you.

If you are not sure what to ask, start from the home page. It lists the topic maps, the explainers, and the calculators.

Replies usually come within a few business days when volume allows. You will get a human note, not an autoresponder sequence. If the question is already answered on a page, we may send you that link.

This is not a tutoring desk and not a homework hotline. We may decline to solve a full assignment. We welcome short, specific questions and topic suggestions. See the FAQ on contact expectations and the disclaimer.

Thank you. Your note is on its way. We will read it and reply when we can.

Messages are used only to reply to you, never sold or shared. Read the privacy policy.
