AP Calculus AB bridge

Pathway · Educational only · Not affiliated with College Board

This pathway assumes comfort with AB-style limits, derivatives, and integrals, and aims at deeper meaning plus readiness for college calculus or self-study multivariable later.

Suggested order

  1. Calculus foundations — rebuild the map in plain language.
  2. What a derivative actually measures — rates, units, linear approximation.
  3. Numerical derivative calculator — feel difference quotients.
  4. Epsilon-delta intuition without terror — optional but high leverage.
  5. Implicit differentiation intuition, then try the numerical derivative calculator on a branch you can solve.
  6. Sequences and series — if your course or next course includes them.
  7. Convergence tests for series when you need comparison, ratio, or root without fog.
  8. Differential equations first look + separable walkthrough.
Practice habit. For each derivative rule you already know, write one sentence naming what quantity’s rate you are computing in a real context (distance, cost, population, temperature).

Glossary anchors: limit, derivative, definite integral.
