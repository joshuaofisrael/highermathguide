Trust and purpose

About Higher Math Guide

A free learning resource for students who want higher mathematics explained with intuition and precision.

Higher Math Guide is an educational project of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. The site publishes original explainers, topic maps, a glossary, study pathways, and small calculators that run in your browser. There are no ads, no affiliate links, and no paid product funnel. The offer is the learning itself.

Mission

Many students meet calculus, linear algebra, probability, and analysis as a fog of symbols. This project writes the same ideas in calm prose, then points to textbooks and open courseware when you want a second voice. Pages are meant to stand alone, then lead you deeper through internal links.

Free to read

Every explainer, pathway, and calculator is available without an account.

Original prose

Articles are written for this site. Citations are for further reading, not copied lectures.

Organic first

The site is built to be found by learners searching for ideas, not for ad clicks.

Original content policy

Explanations, examples, and commentary on this site are original. When a standard theorem or worked pattern appears, it is taught in our own words and checked against reputable sources. We link those sources so you can verify claims and continue study. We do not paste textbook paragraphs or scrape other sites.

If a page ever looks wrong, write through the contact form. Corrections help the next reader.

Who publishes this

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC publishes Higher Math Guide as a public educational project. Contact email is joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. Messages should use the subject tag [Contact: Higher Math Guide] so they are easy to find. The contact form sets that tag for you.

What this is not

This is not a school, a tutoring desk, or an exam board. It is not a substitute for your instructor, syllabus, or professional advice. Read the full educational disclaimer before you rely on any page or calculator.
